<h3>One Big Swing</h3>
<p>Buy-in: $2600</p>
<p>Table Minimum: $5-$25</p>
<p>Notes: Very high outlay going for one big hit before regressing down with profit. Bankroll is for 3 shooters instead of the usual 10.</p>
<ol>
<li>Wait for a Point to be established.</li>
<li>Place $860 Across; Buy the 4 and 10 for $100 each, and Place $150 each on the 5 and 9, and $180 each on the 6 and 8.</li>
<li>This is your one big swing! If any place number hits, immediately regress down to $204 Across; $25 each on the 4 and 10, $35 each on the 5 and 9, and $42 each on the 6 and 8. If the Inside numbers rolled, you'll be playing with a few dollars of profit, and if the 4 or 10 hit, you'll still be in the hand for $9.</li>
<li>Let the bets ride. You'll be collecting $50 for $1 with each hit until a 7 Out. Have a good ending goal so you can walk away with profit.</li>
</ol>
