<h3>Ray's Way</h3>
<p>Buy-in: $600-$1000</p>
<p>Table Minimum: $5-$25</p>
<p>Notes: Strategy that gets you to profit in 4 hits, plus it good for cruise comps.</p>
<ol>
<li>Wait for a Point to be established, or if you are shooting, bet a Do'ey-Don't. with table minimum bets on the Pass and Don't Pass lines.<li>
<li>Place $110 Inside; $25 each on the 5 and 9, $30 each on the 6 and 8.</li>
<li>On the first hit of any number, use the $35 winnings to press the 5 and 9 up to $30 each and the 6 and 8 up to $42 each, collecting $1 in change.</li>
<li>Now if the 6 or 8 hit, use the $49 winnings to press everything up $44 Inside, so you'll have $40 each on the 5 and 9 and $54 each on the 6 and 8.</li>
<li>If the 5 or 9 hit instead, drop in $2 from your rack and perform the same $44 Inside press.</li>
<li>Once you collect the next two hits from any number, you'll be playing with profit.</li>
<li>Let the bets ride until a 7 Out. Use the extra money from the winnings to make bets for the dealers or just tip them outright.</li>
<li>When you are up, start any collect and press system that you like.</li>
</ol>
