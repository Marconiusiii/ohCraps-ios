<h3>Crapless Craps: Yo 68</h3>
<p>Buy-in: $850</p>
<p>Table Minimum: $5-$15</p>
<p>Notes: Play with profit in either 1 or 2 hits.</p>
<ol>
<li>Make a $15 Pass Line bet, and go up on the All Tall Small for $5 each. If the table doesn't have the ATS, don't make any bets and wait for the Point to be established.</li>
<li>Place the 6 and 8 for $30 each, and place and Buy the 11 for $25.</li>
<li>If the 11 hits once, or if the 6 or 8 hit twice, collect the winnings and regress down to $66 Inside; $15 each on the 5 and 9, $18 each on the 6 and 8. You are now fully out of the hand.</li> 
<li>Start any collect and press system that you like, and feel free to start spreading out to the other numbers every other hit.</li>
</ol>
